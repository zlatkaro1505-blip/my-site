BIOGRAPHY

Lana Del Rey is an American singer and songwriter. Her real name is Elizabeth Woolridge Grant. She was born on June 21, 1985, in New York City, USA. She became interested in music at a young age and started writing songs as a teenager. Before becoming famous, she performed in small clubs and worked hard to develop her musical style.

Lana Del Rey became famous in 2011 after releasing her song Video Games. In 2012, she released her second studio album, Born to Die, which brought her international success. Her music combines pop, indie, and alternative styles. She is known for her emotional voice, poetic lyrics, and nostalgic atmosphere. Some of her most popular songs are Summertime Sadness, Young and Beautiful, and West Coast.
